Founder Story

I was born and raised in Denver, Colorado, in a family where gardening was simply part of life. My dad, a former chef, taught me how to cook from a young age. I learned how to grow food from my mom, who always had the biggest garden in the neighborhood. Together, they gave me a deep respect for the entire process behind food, from growing and harvesting to cooking and sharing meals.

I cooked professionally for most of my life until COVID disrupted the industry and forced me to rethink my path. That moment pushed me back toward something I had always been at heart, a nerd who loved understanding how things work. I got a computer engineering degree from MSU Denver, and it opened my eyes to what technology can enable.

The more I learned, the clearer something became to me. We already have the technology and the resources to end world hunger. What we are missing is not capability, but connection, coordination, and accessible systems that empower people to grow and share food locally.

That belief is what led me here.

I am currently building a platform that connects plant shops and nurseries with people in their local neighborhoods. It is a place where anyone can discover plants, learn how to care for them, and connect with others who are passionate about growing things. On the surface, it is a simple website. At its core, it is the foundation for something much bigger.

By building this network, I am developing real relationships across agritourism, agrihoods, sustainability, greenhouse technology, and alternative living spaces such as earthships. Those connections will guide the next phase of this work, developing automated greenhouse technology that supports local food production and makes growing food more accessible, efficient, and resilient.

This project is bootstrapped, intentional, and long term. It is about rebuilding our relationship with food, starting locally, using technology as a tool, and scaling impact in a way that is practical, and sustainable.

If you are supporting this work, you are supporting a future where growing food is easier, more local, and available to everyone.
